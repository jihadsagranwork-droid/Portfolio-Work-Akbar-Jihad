About Me

Professional Summary

Communications and digital marketing professional with over five years of progressively responsible experience spanning national broadcast media, higher-education media production, digital marketing, and multimedia content production across Malaysia, Indonesia, and Timor-Leste.

Combines strategic and institutional communications expertise including government liaison, crisis communications, and reputational risk management with hands-on digital marketing and multimedia production skills, including Meta Ads Manager, Google Analytics, SEO/SEM, and end-to-end video production.

Proven track record of growing digital audiences, managing paid campaigns, producing broadcast and social content, and coordinating cross-functional, multicultural teams. Holds a Master of Media Studies with Distinction from Universiti Malaya, earned under the prestigious MTCP Scholarship 2024, following a Bachelor of Human Sciences in Communication from IIUM. The result: strategy grounded in evidence and creative work built to perform.
